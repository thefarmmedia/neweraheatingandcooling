About New ERA Heating & Cooling

Family-owned, locally operated, and dedicated to serving Springfield since 2009.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

New ERA Heating & Cooling was founded in 2009 by Bryan Swanson — a 30-year HVAC veteran with a Master Mechanical License and NATE certification. Bryan started the company with a single mission: to give Springfield homeowners an HVAC partner they could actually trust.

After spending years watching homeowners get overcharged and under-served by large corporate HVAC companies, Bryan decided to do things differently. New ERA was built on honest pricing, quality workmanship, and treating every customer like a neighbor — because most of them are.

Today, New ERA has grown into one of the most respected HVAC companies in the Springfield metro area. We've served thousands of homes and businesses across 24 communities in southwest Missouri, and we're proud to have earned over 500 five-star reviews along the way.

We're a proud Rheem dealer, which means we install and service one of the most trusted equipment brands in the industry. Every system we install is backed by industry-leading warranties and our own labor guarantee.
