Boiler Service & Repair

Springfield, MO

While less common than forced-air systems in Southwest Missouri, hydronic (hot water and steam) boiler systems provide exceptional, even heat that many homeowners love. If your home is heated by a boiler, New ERA Heating & Cooling provides expert diagnosis, repair, and maintenance services.

Boiler Services We Provide

Boiler Repair: From ignition issues and pilot lights to pressure problems, zone valve failures, and circulator pump replacement — we have experience with residential and light commercial boilers.

Annual Boiler Maintenance: We clean burners, check pressure settings, test safety controls, inspect venting, and verify proper system operation before each heating season.

Boiler Replacement: When a boiler reaches end of life, we can install modern high-efficiency condensing boilers that significantly reduce heating costs.
