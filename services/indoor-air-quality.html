Indoor Air Quality Services

Springfield, MO

The air inside your home can be 2–5x more polluted than outdoor air, according to the EPA. Dust, pollen, mold spores, bacteria, pet dander, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) circulate through your HVAC system and the air you breathe every day.

New ERA Heating & Cooling offers a complete range of indoor air quality solutions to help Springfield homeowners breathe cleaner, healthier air.

Our Indoor Air Quality Solutions

Whole-Home Air Purifiers: Installed directly into your HVAC system, these remove up to 99.9% of airborne particles including dust, pollen, pet dander, mold spores, bacteria, and viruses — far more effective than standard filters.

UV Germicidal Lights: UV-C lights installed in your air handler kill bacteria, mold, and viruses as air passes through. Particularly effective at preventing mold growth on evaporator coils.

Whole-Home Humidifiers: Dry winter air causes discomfort, dry skin, static electricity, and can crack wood floors and furniture. A whole-home humidifier maintains ideal humidity levels automatically.

Advanced Filtration: Upgrade from standard 1-inch filters to thicker media filters (4–5 inch) that capture finer particles without the airflow restrictions of HEPA filters.
