UV Light Air Purification Systems

Springfield, MO

UV germicidal lights use ultraviolet-C (UV-C) radiation to kill mold, bacteria, viruses, and other biological contaminants as air passes through your HVAC system. Originally developed for hospitals and medical facilities, UV air purification is now available for residential HVAC systems at a very affordable price.

How UV Lights Work in HVAC

UV-C lights are mounted inside your air handler, typically near the evaporator coil. As air circulates through your system, it passes through the UV light field — which destroys the DNA of microorganisms and prevents reproduction.

Coil Sterilization: The moist, dark evaporator coil is an ideal breeding ground for mold and bacteria. UV lights keep the coil sanitized, preventing biofilm buildup that reduces efficiency and causes odors.

Air Sterilization: Some systems use multiple UV lamps to treat airborne pathogens as they pass through the air stream — particularly effective in reducing the spread of airborne viruses.
