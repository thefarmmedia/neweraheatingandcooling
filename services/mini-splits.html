Mini-Split & Ductless AC Services

Springfield, MO

Ductless mini-split systems offer flexible, efficient comfort for spaces where running ductwork isn't practical or possible. Whether you're adding a sunroom, converting a garage, or just have a room that never quite gets comfortable, a mini-split is often the best solution.

New ERA Heating & Cooling installs and services single-zone and multi-zone ductless mini-split systems from leading manufacturers including Rheem, Mitsubishi, and Daikin.

Is a Mini-Split Right for You?

Mini-splits are ideal for: home additions without ductwork, garages and workshops, historic homes where duct installation would be disruptive, rooms that are always too hot or cold, rental units requiring independent climate control, and sunrooms or enclosed porches.

Single-Zone Systems: One outdoor unit connected to one indoor air handler — perfect for a single room or space.

Multi-Zone Systems: One outdoor unit connected to multiple indoor air handlers throughout your home — gives independent temperature control to each zone without the cost of installing full ductwork.
