Choosing the Right Furnace

80% AFUE Furnaces: Reliable, affordable single-stage heating. 80% of fuel is converted to heat, 20% is vented. Good choice for budget-conscious replacement.

95–97% AFUE Furnaces: High-efficiency condensing furnaces convert 95–97% of fuel to heat. Significantly lower operating costs and may qualify for rebates. Require PVC venting (not flue pipe).

Two-Stage vs. Single-Stage: Two-stage furnaces run at low capacity (65%) most of the time, only kicking to full blast when needed. This improves humidity control, reduces temperature swings, and cuts heating bills.

Variable-Speed Blowers: These pair with two-stage furnaces for maximum efficiency and comfort — the fan adjusts speed continuously for steady, even heat distribution.
