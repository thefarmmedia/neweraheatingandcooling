Heat Pump Services

Springfield, MO

Heat pumps provide year-round comfort — heating in winter and cooling in summer — from a single, energy-efficient system. Modern heat pumps are significantly more efficient than traditional furnaces and AC units, making them an excellent long-term investment for Springfield homeowners.

New ERA Heating & Cooling installs, services, and repairs all types of heat pump systems, including traditional split systems and modern cold-climate heat pumps that work efficiently even in below-freezing temperatures.

Why Consider a Heat Pump?

Heat pumps work by moving heat rather than generating it, making them 2–3x more energy efficient than electric resistance heating. For homes with natural gas that's subject to price volatility, a heat pump can lock in lower, more predictable heating costs.

Traditional Heat Pumps: Work well in Missouri's climate, which rarely drops below 20°F for extended periods. Ideal for full-home heating and cooling replacement.

Dual-Fuel Systems: Combine a heat pump with a gas furnace for maximum efficiency — the heat pump handles mild weather while the furnace takes over during the coldest days.

Cold-Climate Heat Pumps: Modern inverter-driven heat pumps can operate efficiently down to -15°F, making them viable for cold Missouri winters without a backup furnace.
