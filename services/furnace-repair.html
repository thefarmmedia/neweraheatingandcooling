Common Furnace Problems We Fix

No Heat: Ignition failure, gas valve issues, tripped high limit switch, failed circuit board. We systematically diagnose the cause — not just replace parts randomly.

Short Cycling: Furnace turns on and off repeatedly without heating the home. Common causes: dirty air filter, oversized equipment, heat exchanger issues, or faulty thermostat.

Pilot Light / Ignition Issues: Modern furnaces use hot surface igniters or spark igniters. These wear out over time and are common repair needs.

Blower Motor Problems: If your furnace runs but the fan doesn't blow air — or blows weak air — the blower motor or capacitor may need replacement.

Strange Smells: A burning smell could indicate a dirty filter or overheating issue. A sulfur (rotten egg) smell is a gas leak emergency — evacuate and call immediately.
