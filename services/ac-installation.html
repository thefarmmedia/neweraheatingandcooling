AC Installation in Springfield, MO

Expert installation of new air conditioning systems

Choosing and installing a new air conditioning system is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your home's comfort and energy costs for the next 15–20 years. New ERA Heating & Cooling takes the time to properly size your system, explain your options, and install everything according to manufacturer specifications and local code.

We're a proud Rheem dealer and install systems across all efficiency levels to match your budget and energy goals.

Our AC Installation Process

1. In-Home Evaluation: A NATE-certified technician visits your home to measure the space, inspect ductwork, and perform a Manual J load calculation to determine the correct system size.

2. Equipment Selection: We present Good, Better, and Best options matched to your home's needs and your budget — or use our System Builder tool online for a preliminary estimate.

3. Installation Day: Our crew arrives, removes the old equipment (included), installs the new system, and fully tests it before leaving. Most installations are completed in one day.

4. System Orientation: We walk you through the new equipment, thermostat settings, filter locations, and answer all your questions before we leave.
