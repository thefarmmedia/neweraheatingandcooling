Common Water Heater Problems

No Hot Water: Pilot light out (gas units), failed thermocouple, bad heating element (electric units), or tripped reset button. We diagnose and fix the root cause.

Not Enough Hot Water: Sediment buildup, undersized unit, failing heating element, or thermostat set too low. Often resolved with a flush or thermostat adjustment.

Rusty or Discolored Water: Corrosion inside the tank — often the sacrificial anode rod has been depleted. We replace the anode and flush the tank.

Leaking: A leak from the tank body (not connections) typically indicates tank corrosion and replacement is needed. Leaks from fittings or the T&P relief valve are often repairable.
