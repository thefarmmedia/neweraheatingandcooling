Common AC Problems We Fix

AC Not Cooling: Low refrigerant, dirty coils, failed capacitor, or thermostat issues — we diagnose the root cause and fix it, not just the symptom.

AC Not Turning On: Tripped breaker, failed contactor, blown fuse, or control board issues. We check all electrical components systematically.

AC Running but Warm Air: Low refrigerant charge, frozen evaporator coils, or dirty condenser coils are common culprits. We'll find it fast.

Strange Noises: Squealing, banging, clicking, or rattling sounds usually indicate a mechanical issue that needs prompt attention before it causes more damage.

AC Leaking Water: A clogged condensate drain is the most common cause — often fixed quickly on-site. We also check for refrigerant leaks and coil issues.
