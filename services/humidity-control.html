Whole-Home Humidifier Installation

Springfield, MO

Missouri winters may not be as dry as the desert Southwest, but indoor humidity still drops significantly during heating season. When relative humidity drops below 30%, you feel colder at the same temperature, suffer dry skin and sinuses, and may see wood floors and furniture begin to crack. A whole-home humidifier solves all of this automatically.

Whole-Home vs. Portable Humidifiers

A portable humidifier covers one room and requires constant refilling. A whole-home bypass or flow-through humidifier connects to your water supply line, requires minimal maintenance, and humidifies every room in your home automatically — controlled by a dedicated humidistat or integrated with your smart thermostat.

Flow-Through Humidifiers: Use a continuous stream of water over a water panel, with excess water draining away. More efficient and lower maintenance than drum types.

Steam Humidifiers: Boil water to create steam — the most powerful and accurate whole-home solution, ideal for larger homes or those needing precise humidity control.
