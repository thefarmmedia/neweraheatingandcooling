Furnace Replacement in Springfield, MO

Upgrade to a safer, more efficient heating system

If your furnace is over 15 years old, requiring frequent repairs, or not heating your home evenly, it may be time for a replacement. Modern 96-97% AFUE furnaces can cut your heating bills by 25–40% compared to older 70–80% efficiency units — often paying for themselves within 7–10 years.

New Furnace Benefits

Lower Energy Bills: A 96% AFUE furnace converts 96 cents of every dollar of gas into heat, versus 78 cents for a typical 20-year-old unit. On a $150/month heating bill, that's $27/month in savings.

Better Comfort: Two-stage and modulating furnaces eliminate the temperature swings common with old single-stage systems — keeping every room more consistently comfortable.

Quieter Operation: Modern variable-speed furnaces run much quieter than old single-stage units, which means less disruption to your home life.

Improved Air Quality: New systems pair with advanced filtration and can include built-in air purification or humidity control.
