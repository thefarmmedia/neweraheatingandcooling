AC Tune-Up & Maintenance

Springfield, MO — Annual AC Service

Annual air conditioning maintenance is one of the most cost-effective things you can do as a homeowner. A properly maintained AC system runs 15–20% more efficiently, lasts 5–7 years longer, and is far less likely to break down during the hottest days of summer.

New ERA Heating & Cooling offers thorough annual tune-ups for all makes and models of air conditioning systems throughout the Springfield area.

What Our AC Tune-Up Includes

Our comprehensive AC tune-up typically takes 60–90 minutes and covers all critical components of your air conditioning system:

Electrical Checks: We test voltage, amperage, and resistance on motors and compressor. Worn capacitors and contactors are identified before they fail.

Refrigerant Level Check: Proper refrigerant charge is critical for efficiency and equipment longevity. We check levels and inspect for leaks.

Coil Cleaning: Dirty condenser and evaporator coils can reduce efficiency by 30% or more. We clean both as part of every tune-up.

Drain Line Flush: A clogged condensate drain is one of the most common summer AC problems. We flush the line and treat it with algaecide.
