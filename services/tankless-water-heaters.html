Tankless Water Heater Installation

Springfield, MO

Tankless water heaters (also called on-demand or instantaneous water heaters) eliminate the storage tank entirely — heating water only when you need it. The result is unlimited hot water, up to 30% lower water heating costs, and a unit that lasts twice as long as a conventional tank.

Tankless Water Heater Benefits

Never Run Out of Hot Water: A properly sized tankless unit delivers hot water on demand — no matter how many people are showering, running laundry, or filling the dishwasher simultaneously.

Energy Savings: Traditional water heaters keep 40–80 gallons of water hot 24/7, even when you're sleeping or at work. Tankless units eliminate this standby heat loss, saving 24–34% on water heating costs for average households.

Longer Lifespan: Traditional tanks last 8–12 years. Tankless units regularly last 20–25 years with proper maintenance — and their components are replaceable, unlike a corroded tank.

Space Savings: A tankless unit is about the size of a large briefcase and mounts on the wall, freeing up valuable floor space.
