Air Conditioning Services

Springfield, MO

When the Missouri summer heat hits, you need an air conditioning system you can count on. New ERA Heating & Cooling provides comprehensive AC services to homeowners and businesses throughout the Springfield area — from routine tune-ups to emergency repairs and complete system replacements.

Our NATE-certified technicians service all makes and models of central air conditioning systems, and we're a proud Rheem dealer for equipment installations.

Our Air Conditioning Services

AC Repair: Fast diagnosis and repair of any AC problem — not cooling, not turning on, frozen coils, refrigerant leaks, strange noises, or anything else. We stock common parts on our trucks for same-day repairs.

AC Maintenance / Tune-Up: Annual preventive maintenance extends system life, improves efficiency, and helps prevent breakdowns. We clean coils, check refrigerant, test electrical components, and lubricate moving parts.

AC Installation & Replacement: When it's time for a new system, we help you choose the right size and efficiency level for your home, handle the complete installation, and haul away the old equipment.
