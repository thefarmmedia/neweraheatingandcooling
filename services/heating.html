Heating & Furnace Services

Springfield, MO

Missouri winters demand reliable heating. New ERA Heating & Cooling provides expert furnace services — from emergency repairs when your heat goes out to planned replacements and annual tune-ups that keep your system running efficiently for years.

We service all brands and models of gas furnaces, and as a Rheem dealer, we offer competitive pricing on new equipment installations.

Our Heating Services

Furnace Repair: Fast diagnosis and repair of gas furnace problems — pilot light issues, ignition failures, blower motor problems, cracked heat exchangers, and more. 24/7 emergency service when you lose heat.

Annual Furnace Tune-Up: We thoroughly inspect, clean, and test your furnace every fall before the heating season begins. Regular maintenance can reduce heating costs by 15% and catch problems before they become expensive emergencies.

Furnace Installation & Replacement: If your furnace is over 15 years old, increasingly unreliable, or using expensive R-22 refrigerant, it may be time to replace it. We'll help you choose the right new system and handle complete installation.
