AC Replacement in Springfield, MO

Upgrade to a new, more efficient air conditioning system

Is your air conditioner over 12 years old, increasingly unreliable, or costing more each summer to run? It may be time to replace it. Modern air conditioning systems are 30–50% more efficient than units installed just 15 years ago — and a properly sized, well-installed replacement can dramatically improve comfort while reducing energy bills.

When Is It Time to Replace Your AC?

Age Over 12–15 Years: As AC systems age, efficiency drops, parts become harder to find, and repair costs increase. If your system is approaching 15 years, a replacement is usually more cost-effective than continued repairs.

R-22 Refrigerant: Systems manufactured before 2010 typically use R-22 (Freon), which is now banned and extremely expensive. A refrigerant leak in an R-22 system is often a replacement trigger.

High Repair Costs: If repairs exceed 30–50% of replacement cost (or use the 5,000 rule: age × repair cost > $5,000), replacement is usually smarter.

Inefficiency: If your energy bills have been climbing despite reasonable usage, your aging system may be struggling to operate efficiently.
