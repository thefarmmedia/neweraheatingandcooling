Furnace Tune-Up & Maintenance

Springfield, MO — Annual Fall Furnace Service

Your furnace works hard every Missouri winter. An annual tune-up before the heating season starts ensures it's operating safely, efficiently, and reliably when you need it most. New ERA Heating & Cooling offers comprehensive furnace maintenance throughout the Springfield area.

Why Annual Furnace Maintenance Matters

Safety: A cracked heat exchanger allows carbon monoxide to enter your home's airstream — a deadly, odorless gas. We inspect the heat exchanger on every tune-up.

Efficiency: A clean, well-tuned furnace operates 15–20% more efficiently than a neglected one. Annual maintenance often pays for itself in energy savings.

Reliability: Catching small problems (a worn igniter, loose electrical connection, dirty flame sensor) before they cause a breakdown saves you emergency repair costs and discomfort.

Warranty: Many furnace manufacturers require annual professional maintenance to maintain the parts warranty. Skip a year and you may void coverage on a $500+ repair.
