Choosing Your New Water Heater

Traditional 40–50 Gallon Tank: Best for homes with 2–4 people. Heats water in advance and stores it ready for use. Lower upfront cost, simple operation. Replace every 10–15 years.

Traditional 75–80 Gallon Tank: Best for larger families or homes with high hot water demand. Same technology as smaller tanks, just more capacity.

Tankless (On-Demand): Heats water only when you need it — eliminating standby heat loss. Delivers unlimited hot water, operates more efficiently, and lasts 20+ years. Higher upfront cost but long-term savings.
