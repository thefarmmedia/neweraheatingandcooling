HVAC Electrical Services

Springfield, MO

Proper electrical connections are critical to safe and efficient HVAC operation. New ERA Heating & Cooling handles the electrical components of HVAC installation and service, from disconnect panels to surge protection and smart thermostat wiring.

HVAC Electrical Services We Provide

Disconnect Panels: Every outdoor AC unit or heat pump requires a dedicated disconnect panel for safe service access. We install and upgrade these to current code standards.

Surge Protection: HVAC equipment is expensive. Power surges — whether from lightning or grid issues — can destroy compressors and control boards in an instant. Whole-system surge protection is a smart, low-cost investment.

Smart Thermostat Wiring: Many older homes require a C-wire (common wire) for smart thermostat operation. We add C-wire adapters or run new thermostat wire to make the upgrade seamless.

Capacitor & Contactor Service: These are among the most common reasons for AC and heat pump failures. We stock these parts on our trucks for same-day repair.
