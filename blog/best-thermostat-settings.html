Best Thermostat Settings for Missouri Summers and Winters

March 22, 2025  ·  Bryan Swanson  ·  4 min read

Your thermostat settings are one of the simplest levers you have for controlling your energy bill. Small changes add up significantly over a Missouri summer or winter. Here's what we recommend.

Summer Settings (Cooling Season)

The U.S. Department of Energy recommends:

  • When home: 78°F
  • When sleeping: 82°F
  • When away: 85°F or higher

Every degree above 72°F saves approximately 3% on cooling costs. If you're currently keeping it at 72°F all day, moving to 78°F saves roughly 18% — that's a meaningful reduction on a summer electric bill that might run $200–$300/month.

Key tip: Ceiling fans help significantly. With a fan running, you can raise the thermostat 4°F with no perceived comfort loss. Just remember to turn fans off when you leave a room — fans cool people, not rooms.

Winter Settings (Heating Season)

The DOE winter recommendations:

  • When home and awake: 68°F
  • When sleeping: 60–65°F
  • When away: 60–65°F

Most people sleep better in a cooler room anyway. Lowering the thermostat 7–10 degrees for 8 hours per day (sleeping and away) can save about 10% on your annual heating costs.

The Case for Smart Thermostats

If you're still using a manual thermostat, a programmable or smart upgrade pays for itself quickly. Smart thermostats like the Rheem EcoNet:

  • Learn your schedule automatically after a few days
  • Adjust to weather forecasts (pre-cooling before hot afternoons)
  • Allow remote control from your phone when plans change
  • Show energy usage history so you can see the impact of changes
  • Can integrate directly with Rheem HVAC equipment for maximum efficiency

The average smart thermostat saves $100–$200 per year on energy costs — enough to pay for itself within the first year.

What About Humidity?

Missouri summers are famously humid, and humidity makes temperatures feel hotter than they are. The "feels like" temperature at 78°F and 70% humidity might feel like 84°F. A whole-home dehumidifier can let you maintain a higher thermostat setting comfortably. If you're fighting humidity, ask us about Rheem's whole-home dehumidification options.
