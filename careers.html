Why Work at New ERA?

A Place Where Technicians Are Valued

At New ERA Heating & Cooling, we believe our technicians are our most important asset. We invest in training, pay competitively, and create a work environment where people actually enjoy coming to work.

Owner Bryan Swanson started as a technician himself — he knows what the job is like and what it takes to do it right. We treat every team member with the same respect and honesty we show our customers.

Competitive Pay

We pay top market rates for experienced technicians, with performance bonuses and regular reviews.

Training & Certification Support

We pay for NATE certification, ongoing training, and manufacturer certifications to advance your career.

Great Team Culture

Small team environment where everyone knows each other. No corporate nonsense — just good people doing great work.

Company Vehicle & Tools

Take-home company vehicles and top-quality tools provided. No more hauling your own equipment.
